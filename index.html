About

I am a Researcher at Ant Group, currently working on Agentic RL and Coding Agents. My research focuses on enabling LLM-based agents to learn from interaction, diagnose their own failures, and continuously improve their capabilities in real-world environments. Previously, I worked on foundation model pre-training and large language model alignment, including post-training and preference alignment. I received my PhD from The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen in 2026, advised by Dr. Xiang Wan and Prof. Tsung-Hui Chang.

I am always open to research collaborations and discussions! Please drop me with an email.
