About

Hi, I am Zhuo Li, a 5th-year PhD student at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, supervised by Dr. Xiang Wan and Prof. Tsung-Hui Chang . I obtained a B.E. degree in Data Science from the School of Computer Science and Engineering, Central South University, Changsha, China, in 2020.

Interests

My research interests lie in data-centric machine learning and its combinations with real-world applications. I am interested in data manipulation, probabilistic methods, and representation learning. These developed methods have been applied to robust long-tailed vision recognition, efficient data augmentation and weighting, sample selection and distillation, multimodal representation learning, and NLP. Recently, I am focused on developing methods for better alignment of LLM with downstream tasks and human preferences, such as automatic prompt engineering, data curation, reward modeling and RLHF algorithm.
