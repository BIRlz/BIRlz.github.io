About Me

Hi, I am Zhuo Li. I obtained my PhD at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, supervised by Dr. Xiang Wan and Prof. Tsung-Hui Chang in 2026. I obtained a B.E. degree in Data Science from Central South University in 2020. My research interests lie in data-centric machine learning and probabilistic methods. Currently, I am focused on aligning Large Language Models (LLMs) with downstream tasks and human preferences, including Reward Modeling and RLHF.
